# Golden Pixel support

Golden Pixel is a free pixel-art idle brick breaker for iPhone, made for the inner screen of
iPhone Duo.

## Get help or report a bug

Open an issue at
[github.com/0x63616c/golden-pixel/issues](https://github.com/0x63616c/golden-pixel/issues).
Say which iPhone you play on, the iOS version, and the version and build number shown at the
bottom of the main menu.

## How to play

1. Tap **PLAY**.
2. Pick a ball in the panel on the right.
3. Hold an open, dark spot on the board, pull back like a slingshot and let go. The ball
   flies the other way and keeps bouncing.
4. Balls break the blue blocks and pay coins. Paler blue means fewer hits left.
5. Spend coins on more balls. Spend mint shards in the **TREE** on skills.
6. Find the golden pixel and break it to finish the level.

## Questions

**Does it need the internet?** No. It plays fully offline.

**Where is my progress saved?** On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**Why are there black bars on my iPhone?** The game is drawn on an exact pixel grid sized for
iPhone Duo. On other iPhones it uses the largest whole-number scale that fits, so pixels stay
sharp, and the rest is black.

**Does it cost anything?** No. Golden Pixel is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

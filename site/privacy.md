# Golden Pixel privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Golden Pixel is a free game for iPhone. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

## What we collect

Nothing. Golden Pixel does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data, and it does
not track you across apps or websites.

- **No network access.** The game never connects to the internet.
- **No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising** and no third-party code that could
  collect data.
- **No device identifiers.** The game does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) or any
  other identifier.

## Your progress

Your game progress is saved in a small file on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. It
never leaves the device except as part of your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which
Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes the save.

## Children

Golden Pixel collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds online features), this page will
be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.

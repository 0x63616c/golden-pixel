## Replies you'll need

- **"What's the catch?"** No catch: it's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. I made the
  game I wanted to play.
- **"Android?"** iPhone only for now.
- **"iPad?"** Not yet; it's built around one pixel grid per screen, and iPad needs its own.
- **"Is it really idle?"** Yes: balls bounce forever and you earn while away. The choices are
  where to place balls and what to buy.
- **"Does progress carry over?"** Each level starts fresh, but every golden pixel you break is
  a permanent star: +5% damage and +10% coins on every level after.

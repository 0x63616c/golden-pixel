# Hashtags and tags

Fewer is better. Pick from the set that matches the post's angle; don't stack them all.

## X (1 to 2 per post, at the end)

- Launch / store: `#iOSGames` `#indiegame`
- Idle angle: `#idlegame` `#incrementalgame`
- Craft angle: `#pixelart` `#gamedev` `#indiedev`
- Community days: `#screenshotsaturday` (Saturday, with a GIF or clip), `#PixelArt` +
  `#gamedev` on dev-thread posts

## Bluesky

Hashtags work but matter less than feeds. Use 0 to 2: `#pixelart`, `#gamedev`, `#indiedev`.
Post craft clips where pixel-art and game-dev feeds pick them up.

## TikTok (3 to 5)

`#idlegame` `#pixelart` `#mobilegames` `#iphonegames` `#satisfying` (for script 1),
`#indiegame` `#gamedev` (for script 3), `#brickbreaker` `#incrementalgames` (for script 2).

## YouTube Shorts

Title ends with `#shorts`. Add 2 to 3: `#idlegame` `#pixelart` `#iosgames`.

## Instagram Reels

5 to 8 at the end of the caption: `#pixelart` `#pixelartgame` `#indiegame` `#idlegame`
`#mobilegame` `#iphonegames` `#gamedev` `#retrogaming`.

## Reddit

No hashtags. Use the subreddit's flair instead (see `reddit.md`).

## Product Hunt topics (if used)

Games, iOS, Indie Games.

## Avoid

Generic reach-bait (`#viral`, `#fyp` stacks), `#AIart` or anything AI, and tags for platforms
the game isn't on (`#android`, `#ipad`).

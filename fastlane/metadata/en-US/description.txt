Somewhere in the dark, one golden pixel is pulsing. Dig your way to it.

Golden Pixel is a free idle brick breaker drawn entirely on a pixel grid. Every level starts as a small pocket of light inside a buried map. Your balls bounce through the blocks one pixel at a time, every break pushes the light further, and the gold waits somewhere out there.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No tracking.

LAUNCH BALLS, WATCH THEM DIG
• Hold an open spot, pull back like a slingshot and let go: the ball flies the other way and never stops bouncing.
• Seven ball types to unlock: Basic, Fast, Heavy, Volt, Bomb, Sniper and Ghost.
• Every block you break pays coins. Spend them on more balls and watch the board fill with color.

READ THE BOARD
• Blocks are one shade of blue, and the shade is how tough they are: deep blue takes many hits, pale blue is one hit from breaking.
• Fog of war: you only see three cells past open space. The gold glows faintly through the dark, and nearby shards shimmer mint.
• Pinch to zoom out over the whole dig, or in close on the balls.

GROW A SKILL TREE
• Mint shards buried in the rock buy skills: more damage, faster balls, critical hits, more coins.
• Lantern pushes back the dark, Radar boxes in the gold, Sonar pings through the rock.
• Every ball type has its own branch of upgrades and two specializations.

BREAK THE GOLD
• The golden pixel is the level's boss: hundreds of hits, draining as it takes damage.
• Every gold you break is a star for good: more damage and more coins on every level after.
• Seven named levels, then the deep levels keep going.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
Built for the big inner screen of iPhone Duo: every pixel lands exactly on the grid, with no blur and no smoothing. It plays on every other iPhone too.

A CALM SOUNDTRACK
A relaxed menu theme and a shuffled playlist of ten tracks that fade into each other while you dig. Switch the music off in Settings anytime.

KEEPS DIGGING WHILE YOU'RE AWAY
Your balls keep earning while the app is closed, so there is always something waiting when you come back.

Your progress is saved on your device only. Golden Pixel collects no data.

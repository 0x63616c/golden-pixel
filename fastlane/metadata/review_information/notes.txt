2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Golden Pixel is a single-player casual game, an idle brick breaker. The player launches balls that bounce through a buried map and break its blocks one pixel at a time. Every block pays coins for more balls, every break pushes the fog back, and the level ends when the balls find and break its golden pixel. Its value is entertainment: a calm game for short sessions with no ads, no purchases and no accounts. It is for a general audience of casual and idle-game players (age rating 4+). It is not directed at children and is not in the Kids category.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
There is nothing to set up: no account, sign-in, demo credentials, sample files or network connection. Every feature is available offline from the first launch.
- Open the app (landscape only) and tap PLAY. HOW TO PLAY on the menu explains the rules.
- Hold an open (dark, empty) spot on the board, pull back and release to launch a ball. Balls keep bouncing by themselves, break the blue blocks and earn coins.
- Buy more balls from the panel on the right. Tap a ball there for its info card.
- Mint shards found in the rock buy skills: tap TREE.
- Pinch to zoom, drag to move the camera.
- Break the golden pixel to finish the level. LEVELS on the menu replays any level reached.
- The pause button leads to RESUME, RESTART LEVEL and MAIN MENU. SETTINGS has SOUND, MUSIC, HAPTICS, GLOW and RESET PROGRESS.
The app is iPhone only. It is designed for the inner display of iPhone Duo and lays the same interface out on every other iPhone.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs: no data providers, authentication, payment processors, analytics, ads or AI services. It uses Apple system frameworks only. Progress is saved in a file on the device, and no data is collected.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app has no region-specific features or content and works the same in every region. It is in English only.
